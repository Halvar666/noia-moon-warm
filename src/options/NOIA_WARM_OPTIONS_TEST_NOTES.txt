Noia Moon Warm Options 2.1.2a9 — test build (2026-11-07)

- The startup XPCOM component was independently rewritten and now uses an MPL-2.0 notice.
- The maintainer's 64x64 warm icon is used for the Add-ons Manager.
- XPCOM identity, preference names, theme guards, palette choices and other UI behavior are preserved.
- 13 automated mock tests passed. The maintainer reported no visible regressions in Pale Moon 35 (Linux).
- The original Noia Moon Options and Warm Options must NOT be enabled simultaneously.

Both complete themes may stay installed. Enable only the Options add-on matching
the active theme, then restart Pale Moon. Neither Options must uninstall the other.

Theme 1.0.0b1 also fixes the four formerly blue tree-expander PNG assets.
